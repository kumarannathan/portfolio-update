Common questions

The reading looks off compared to my camera

Phone cameras and in-camera meters don't always agree, and neither is "right". Open Settings → Calibration, meter the same even-lit scene with both, and move the offset until Lumen matches the meter you trust. Also check the film: Lumen reads at the box ISO unless you set compensation.

Why is my shutter speed longer than the EV suggests?

Past one second, film loses sensitivity (reciprocity failure). Lumen applies the correction curve the film maker publishes and shows the corrected time, with the uncorrected metered time underneath. Kodak and Fujifilm stocks have no published curve, so those times stay uncorrected.

Lumen asks for the camera. Does it take photos?

No. The camera is the sensor the meter reads from. Nothing is captured, saved or uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. An account only syncs your saved kits between devices. Without one, kits stay on the phone and everything else works the same.

I bought Lumen Pro on another phone

Open Settings → Pro → Restore purchase on the new phone, signed into the same Apple ID. Pro is tied to your Apple ID, not to a Lumen account.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and every synced kit are removed immediately and permanently. If you can't get into the app, email me from the address on the account and I'll delete it for you.

Can you add a film?

Probably. Email the stock, its box ISO, and a link to the maker's reciprocity data if there is any.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Lumen Pro is a one-time in-app purchase; the meter, the films and the dial are free.
