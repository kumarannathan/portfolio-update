How it works

Meter. Set the camera. Shoot.

The camera on your phone is the meter. Everything else is the arithmetic you used to do in your head.

01

Point and read

A live reading in EV, average or spot. Tap Hold to lock it while you recompose.

02

Pick your film

Ilford, Kodak, Fujifilm, Kentmere, CineStill. The ISO is set, and so is the reciprocity curve for exposures that run long.

03

Drag the dial

Lock the aperture and the shutter follows, or the other way round. Full, half or third stops, your call.

Details

The parts that matter on a tripod at dusk.

Long exposures are where meters go wrong. Lumen does the maths film makers publish and most apps skip.

Reciprocity, built in

FP4+ at a metered 30 seconds is really a minute and a half. Lumen shows the corrected time, and how much it added.

ND filters to 20 stops

ND2 to ND1000 and beyond. Minutes-long exposures come out exact, not "about two minutes".

Saved kits

Camera, lens and film as one preset. Switch between the Nikon with HP5 and the Hasselblad with Portra in a tap.

Compensation and calibration

Dial in exposure compensation, and an offset so Lumen agrees with the meter or camera you already trust.

Haptics on every stop

The dial clicks under your thumb, so you can set it without looking down.

Account optional

Sign in to sync kits across devices. Don't, and everything stays on the phone.

Lumen Pro

One purchase. Yours for good.

The meter is free. Pro adds the two things long-exposure shooters asked for, and it never renews.

  • ND filters. Meter through anything from ND2 to 20 stops, with exact long exposures.
  • Saved kits. Camera, lens and film setups one tap away, synced to your account.
  • Support an independent app. Everything else in Lumen stays free.
Privacy

The camera is a meter, not a camera.

Lumen reads light. It never saves or uploads a frame.

  • No photos are stored. The preview is only there so you can see what you're metering.
  • No account is needed. With one, the only thing synced is your list of kits.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Purchases go through the App Store. Lumen never sees a card.
