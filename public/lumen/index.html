How it meters

Three moves. No menus.

The camera on your phone is the meter. Everything else is the arithmetic you used to do in your head.

01 — READ

Point and hold

A live reading in EV, average or spot. Tap Hold to lock it while you recompose, or while the light is changing.

02 — FILM

Pick the stock

The ISO is set, and so is the reciprocity curve for exposures that run long. Thirty films from Ilford, Kodak, Fujifilm, Kentmere and CineStill.

03 — DIAL

Drag to the exposure

Lock the aperture and the shutter follows, or the other way round. Full, half or third stops, with a click under your thumb at every one.

Long exposures

Where other meters stop being honest.

Past a second, film slows down. Lumen does the maths the makers publish and most apps skip.

×1.26
Reciprocity, per stock

FP4+ at a metered 30 seconds is really 73. HP5+ is 87. Lumen shows the corrected time and how much it added.

ND1000
Filters to 20 stops

ND2 through ND1000 and beyond. A two-minute exposure comes out as two minutes, not "about two".

±0.0
Calibration

Compare against a meter or camera you trust, then nudge the offset until the readings agree.

EC
Compensation

Exposure compensation on the readout, so overexposing Portra by a stop is one tap, not a recalculation.
