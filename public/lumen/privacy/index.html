Lumen for iPhone

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE 21 OCTOBER 2026

The short version. Lumen uses your camera to measure light and never saves or uploads a frame. It has no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The only data it ever stores on a server is the list of camera setups you choose to sync, and only if you create an account. You can delete that account, and everything with it, from inside the app.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Lumen app for iPhone ("Lumen", "the app"), published by Kumaran Nathan ("I", "me"). It applies to the app and to this website. It does not cover the App Store itself, which is run by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

The camera

Lumen asks for camera access because the camera is the light meter. The live preview is analysed on the device to produce an exposure reading. No photo or video is ever captured, stored, or sent anywhere. You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera; the app will then show no reading.

What stays on your phone

Everything Lumen needs to work lives on your device: your chosen film, aperture range, stop increments, calibration offset, haptics preference, and, if you are not signed in, your saved kits. None of it leaves the phone.

If you create an account (optional)

An account exists for one reason: to sync your saved kits (camera, lens and film setups) across your devices. You never need one to use the meter. If you do sign up, this is what is stored:

DataWhyWhere
Email addressTo sign you in and to send a password reset if you ask for oneSupabase (authentication)
Name, if you give one or share it through Sign in with AppleShown in the app's Account sectionSupabase (authentication)
PasswordStored only as a salted hash; I cannot read itSupabase (authentication)
Saved kits: a name, film, ISO, ND stops, compensation and aperture rangeSo the same kits appear on every device you sign intoSupabase (database)

Supabase is the hosting provider for this data. Each row is tied to your account and protected so that only you can read or change it. Supabase's own policy is at supabase.com/privacy.

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may give Lumen a private relay email address instead of your real one. That is fine: Lumen only needs an address it can sign you in with.

Purchases

Lumen Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Lumen never sees your payment details. Apple tells the app whether the purchase is active, and that is all. Restoring a purchase works the same way, through your Apple ID.

What Lumen does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other permission beyond the camera.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone. The only third party is Supabase, which hosts account data on my behalf.
  • No data is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Open Lumen, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and every kit stored with it, immediately and permanently. Kits that exist only on the phone are removed when you delete the app. You can also email me (below) and I will delete your account by hand.

Children

Lumen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Changes that affect what is collected will also be noted in the app's release notes.
