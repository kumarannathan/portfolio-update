Lumen for iPhone

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE 23 OCTOBER 2026

The short version. Lumen uses your camera to measure light, and the meter never saves or uploads a frame. Photos you take with the film camera are saved to your own photo library and go nowhere else. It has no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The only data it ever stores on a server is the list of camera setups you choose to sync, and only if you create an account. You can delete that account, and everything with it, from inside the app.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Lumen app for iPhone ("Lumen", "the app"), published by Kumaran Nathan ("I", "me"). It applies to the app and to this website. It does not cover the App Store itself, which is run by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

The camera

Lumen asks for camera access because the camera is the light meter. The live preview is analysed on the device to produce an exposure reading. The meter never captures, stores or sends a photo or video. You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera; the meter and the film camera will then show no picture.

The film camera

The film camera is optional. When you press its shutter, Lumen takes a photo, applies the film look on your phone, and saves the result to your photo library. Each photo's details record which film look you used.

To save, Lumen asks for permission to add photos to your library. That lets it save new photos but not see the ones already there. Photos are never uploaded, synced to your account, or sent to me. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

What stays on your phone

Everything Lumen needs to work lives on your device: your chosen film, aperture range, stop increments, calibration offset, haptics preference, the film you last loaded in the film camera, and, if you are not signed in, your saved kits. None of it leaves the phone.

If you create an account (optional)

An account exists for one reason: to sync your saved kits (camera, lens and film setups) across your devices. You never need one to use the meter. If you do sign up, this is what is stored:

Purchases

Lumen Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Lumen never sees your payment details. Apple tells the app whether the purchase is active. If you are signed in when you buy, the app attaches your account's random ID to the purchase so it can be matched to your account; the ID says nothing about who you are. Restoring a purchase works the same way, through your Apple ID.

What Lumen does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts or microphone, and no access to the photos already in your library. Beyond the camera, the only permission Lumen asks for is to add the photos you take with the film camera.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone. The only third party is Supabase, which hosts account data on my behalf.
  • No data is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Open Lumen, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and every kit stored with it, immediately and permanently. Kits that exist only on the phone are removed when you delete the app. Photos you took with the film camera live in your photo library; delete them there like any other photo. You can also email me (below) and I will delete your account by hand.

Children

Lumen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Changes that affect what is collected will also be noted in the app's release notes.
