Lumen for iPhone

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE 23 OCTOBER 2026

The short version. Lumen uses your camera to measure light, and the meter never saves or uploads a frame. Photos you take with the film camera stay on your phone, in Lumen's own library and, if you choose, your photo library. They go nowhere else. The light readings you save, and where you took them, stay on your phone as well. It has no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The only data it ever stores on a server is the list of camera setups you choose to sync, and only if you create an account. You can delete that account, and everything with it, from inside the app.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Lumen app for iPhone ("Lumen", "the app"), published by Kumaran Nathan ("I", "me"). It applies to the app and to this website. It does not cover the App Store itself, which is run by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

The camera

Lumen asks for camera access because the camera is the light meter. The live preview is analysed on the device to produce an exposure reading. The meter never captures, stores or sends a photo or video. You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera; the meter and the film camera will then show no picture.

The film camera

The film camera is optional. When you press its shutter, Lumen takes a photo and applies the film look on your phone. The photo is kept in Lumen's library, on the roll (album) you have loaded. The library is stored only on this phone, inside the app. Each photo also records which film look you used and when you took it.

Saving to Photos is optional. The "Also save to Photos" switch in the library is on unless you turn it off. While it's on, Lumen also saves a copy of each photo to your photo library. For this, Lumen asks for permission to add photos, which lets it save new photos but not see the ones already there. You can turn the switch off in the library, or remove the permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Photos still save to Lumen's library either way.

Film camera photos are never uploaded, synced to your account, or sent to me. Shared photos go only where you send them with the share button.

Your readings and where you took them

Each time you press the meter's shutter button, Lumen saves the reading to its readings log: the time, the exposure settings, your film and filter, and the session you put it in, if any. The log is stored only on this phone, inside the app.

Saving locations is optional. So you can find readings by place and on a map, Lumen asks to use your location while the app is open. It reads your location once each time you save a reading, never in the background. You can turn this off with "Save locations" in the readings log, or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Readings still save without a place.

To show a place name such as "Golden Gate Park, San Francisco", Lumen sends the reading's coordinates to Apple's map service, which returns the name. The map itself is Apple Maps. Apple handles both under its own privacy policy, and they aren't linked to your Lumen account. Readings and their locations are never uploaded to me or synced to your account.

What stays on your phone

Everything Lumen needs to work lives on your device: your chosen film, aperture range, stop increments, calibration offset, haptics preference, the film you last loaded in the film camera, your rolls and the photos on them, your readings log with its sessions and locations, and, if you are not signed in, your saved kits. None of it leaves the phone.

Purchases

Lumen Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Lumen never sees your payment details. Apple tells the app whether the purchase is active. If you are signed in when you buy, the app attaches your account's random ID to the purchase so it can be matched to your account; the ID says nothing about who you are. Restoring a purchase works the same way, through your Apple ID.

What Lumen does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No contacts or microphone, no location in the background, and no access to the photos already in your library. Beyond the camera, Lumen asks only for your location while the app is open, to tag readings, and for permission to add the photos you take with the film camera.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone. The only third party that stores your data is Supabase, which hosts account data on my behalf.
  • No data is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Open Lumen, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and every kit stored with it, immediately and permanently. Kits that exist only on the phone are removed when you delete the app. To delete film camera photos, delete a photo or a whole roll in Lumen's library; deleting the app removes the library too. Copies saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them in Photos. To delete readings, swipe on a reading or delete a session in the readings log; deleting the app removes the whole log and every location in it. You can also email me (below) and I will delete your account by hand.

Children

Lumen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I will remove it.

Changes
